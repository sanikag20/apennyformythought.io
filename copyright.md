## Project Philosophy

This project is not intended to suggest that every piece of writing needs to become software.

Instead, it explores a question that sits between two parts of my life:

> What happens when something as subjective as writing is approached with the curiosity of an engineer?

The writing comes first.

The technology is simply another way of looking at it.
