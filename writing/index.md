# Writing Index

A chronological and thematic index of writing published through **A Penny for My Thought? A Penny for Mine.**

The live versions of these pieces can be read on the publication website.

---

## Archive

### Went back to my Ibiza.

A reflection on returning to something familiar and discovering that returning does not necessarily mean finding the same place you left.

---

### Not Like Other Girls.

An exploration of identity, comparison, and the strange pressure to distinguish yourself while simultaneously wanting to belong.

---

### World's Smallest Violin

A reflection on perspective, emotion, and the stories we tell ourselves about our own problems.

---

### Caught in a landslide

A piece about movement, uncertainty, and the feeling of being carried along by circumstances that are larger than yourself.

---

### Like Logs in a River

A meditation on people, relationships, and the strange ways lives can intersect without necessarily moving in the same direction.

---

### It's Wednesday My Dudes

A lighter entry that plays with the absurdity and repetition of ordinary life.

---

### Yours Truly, Debby Downer.

A self-aware exploration of pessimism, perspective, and the tendency to find something wrong even when everything appears to be fine.

---

### Almost there ft. Inner Peace

A reflection on progress, patience, and the uncomfortable space between where you are and where you think you should be.

---

### Lost.

A piece about uncertainty, direction, and what it means to not have an answer yet.

---

## Reading the Archive

These pieces do not necessarily belong to neat categories.

That is intentional.

The same essay can be interpreted through multiple themes, and some of the most interesting relationships between pieces may only become visible when the archive is examined as a whole.

A future version of this index may therefore move beyond chronological organization toward semantic and thematic navigation.
